05

To confirm

  1. Tax: included. Confirmed with the hotel: the $46,372 includes VAT 16% and city tax 3%, though the agreement's tax lines only show dashes (p. 1). Worth having that in writing.
  2. Gala and cocktail party. Written as “TBA, $0.00” for 80 dinner guests (p. 1). Get in writing that both are included, for how many guests (we expect 65–70), which drinks, and for how long.
  3. Tips and service. The service-charge line is blank, but tips on food and drink are mandatory under the union (p. 2 §4); no percentage is given. At 15% of the modelled food and drink that is roughly $1,900–2,700.
  4. Breakfast. The homepage says breakfast is included when booking direct; the 5–7 Jan booking page sells the same rate as “Accommodation only” with breakfast extra. Toggle it in the “Is it fair?” section.
  5. Residents. The agreement implies 56 (28 bungalows × 2), but our roster has about 47 adults in rooms, plus two small children in a hotel that is adults only (16+). The price is per bungalow either way.
  6. Ocean View. The block has 0 Ocean View bungalows, yet the hotel sells them. Check that the buyout is truly exclusive.
  7. Paperwork. Payment dates say 1/10/2025 and 1/12/2025 and the signing deadline 9 July 2025 (p. 3); the bank account is in pesos with no exchange rate stated. The three deposits add up to the $46,372 total.

Group-price context

The hotel is currently selling our same room mix for 11–13 Jan at including tax with the 40% sale (prepaid, non-refundable; on Ownia: book 13–30 Nov, stays to 20 Dec 2027, no blackout published, though 5–7 Jan shows no discount). A buyout listing that is very likely Cocolia, “Jungle Retreat Oaxaca Coast”, 28 rooms, quotes from $7,400 a night with half board, about $22,200 for three nights (single occupancy; dates and tax not stated). So the package looks fair against retail prices, but the hotel can clearly sell for less.
